Before you download

Works with
Paladins on Windows, from Steam, Epic or the Hi-Rez standalone install. Nothing else to install.
Updates

Is it safe?

The mods

Switch mods on or off in the launcher. In the game, F1 opens each mod's settings.

RealmBoard

See who you are playing with and against.

Scoreboard
Replaces the Tab board: both teams in one table with damage, healing, shielding, objective time, items and ultimate charge, and the best value of each column highlighted.
Champion select board
Both teams' names, ratings and picks, on Tab.
Enemy names
The enemies' names in champion select, where the game shows "Enemy 1" to "Enemy 5".
Parties
Players who queued together share a color, on the board and in champion select.
Voice
Turn your microphone on or off, and mute teammates or set each one's volume.
How to use it
  1. The scoreboard is on. In a match, hold Tab: the board opens in place of the game's own, and closes when you let go. While it is open, R swaps the items for the five cards.
  2. To change it, press F1, open Realm Board, then Settings. Scoreboard on TAB switches it off; below it are Highlight best values, Summary bar, Background blur, Talent icons and Hide the game's HUD.
  3. In champion select, press Tab for the board of both teams. Change its Toggle key in Settings.
  4. Open the Voice tab to switch your microphone and to mute or set the volume of each teammate who is in voice.

RealmForge

Change how your champion looks, on your screen only.

Skins
Wear body, weapon and mount skins on the champion you play. Companions such as pets and turrets wear the look you save for them.
Effects
Effects from other skin lines, for all of a champion's effects or per ability.
Colors
Color layers on a skin's materials: a fixed color, a fade, a hue cycle, a flicker, or a color that follows your ultimate, health or ammo.
Custom
Wear skins from Tempest .tempest files that you import in the launcher.
Profiles
Named profiles for each champion, worn again each time you spawn.
Champion select
Shows your pick in the skins you chose.
How to use it
  1. In the game press F1 and open Realm Forge. Its tabs are Skins, Effects and Colors, each with your champion and its profile at the top.
  2. On Skins, pick a body, weapon and mount skin. They load and wear while you play, in a match or in the Shooting Range.
  3. On Effects, choose another skin line's effects for all effects or for a single ability.
  4. On Colors, add a layer, choose the part and its source, then press Show me to flash it on your champion. Colors show in a match or the Shooting Range, not in champion select; try them in the range first.
  5. To wear a Tempest skin, open the launcher's Skins page, press Import a Tempest Skin and choose the .tempest file. It shows at once, even with the game running, in the Skins tab's weapon list with an Imported tag: click it to wear it. Custom skins are worn in the Shooting Range and in matches.
  6. Your saved look is worn again each time you spawn. Switch that off in Settings if you don't want it.

Nobody else sees your skins. The mod sends nothing to the server.

RealmQueue

Queue for a match without leaving the Shooting Range.

Queue
Pick a queue from the range and keep practicing until the match is found.
Accept
A found match is accepted for you. You can turn that off and accept it yourself.
Champion select
It appears over the range: pick and lock a champion there, or name one to be picked for you.
Map
Choose which map the range loads.
After the match
After a match you queued for from the range, the game goes back to that range map by itself.
How to use it
  1. In the lobby or the Shooting Range press F1 and open Realm Queue. On the Queue tab the training and casual queues are listed by the game's own titles, such as Siege Training and Onslaught Training.
  2. Choose a queue and press Join Queue. The page shows how long you have been queued and what comes next; Leave Queue cancels.
  3. Keep practicing. When a casual or training match is found it is accepted for you, also for a queue you joined from the game's Play screen; ranked is never accepted for you. With Accept found matches off, press Accept Match on the page, because the range shows no prompt.
  4. Champion select opens over the range: pick a champion, change your mind as often as you like, and Lock In. Without Lock In, your last pick locks shortly before the timer runs out. Set Champion to pick to have one picked for you.
  5. The Shooting Range page lists the maps; choose one and press Go to Map. Change Champion there switches your champion in the range. After a match you queued for from the range, the game returns to that map by itself; switch Return to the range off in Settings if you would rather not.

RealmAudio

Load voice and sound packs beside the game.

Packs
A folder of .pck files, a single .pck or a Tempest .tempest mod. A voice language is a pack too.
Order
Several packs can be on at once. Where two replace the same sound, the one on top wins.
Beside the game
Nothing in the game's folder is overwritten, and only you hear the change. Switch a pack off and the installed sounds come back.
How to use it
  1. In the game press F1, open Realm Audio and press Open Packs Folder. It is Data/RealmAudio/Packs inside your RealmSuite folder.
  2. Put your pack in it and press Rescan Folder. The pack appears in the list.
  3. Switch the pack on. Use the arrows to put the pack you want to win on top.
  4. Hear it from the next time its sounds load: load another champion or start a match. A sound that is already loaded keeps playing as it was, and some menu sounds may not change.

RealmBoost

More frames and less waiting, each change measured.

A cheaper HUD
The HUD does less work every frame: about 7% more frames in the Shooting Range with the cap off.
Frame cap
Keep the game's 228 FPS limit, remove it, or set your own, with a separate one for menus and a key that toggles it.
Alt-tab
Leaving fullscreen is an ordinary window switch, not a freeze.
No 30-second hitch
The game's garbage collection waits while you play and runs on your death camera instead.
Faster start
With the launcher's Fast start, Play reaches the lobby in about 12 seconds instead of 32.
Live
Settings apply on the next frame. Switch one off and the game gets its own value back.
How to use it
  1. RealmBoost starts out leaving the game's own cap as it is. In the game press F1, open Realm Boost, then Settings.
  2. Under Frame cap, set In matches to Off, or to Custom and a Match limit (FPS). It covers matches, the Shooting Range and loading.
  3. Set In menus if you want the lobby and champion select to run at another rate; with Custom, set a Menu limit (FPS).
  4. Choose a Toggle key to turn the cap off and back on while you play.
  5. The game's garbage collection, a short hitch every 30 seconds, waits while you play and runs when your champion dies, where you don't see it. Switch Garbage collection when it cannot be seen off in Settings to keep the game's timing.

Measured on one PC. The start: 12 runs each way, with a saved sign-in. The frames: in the Shooting Range without a cap, where input also reached the screen in 1.7 ms instead of 2.3 ms (median). More frames mean more CPU and GPU work and power.

RealmCraft

Minecraft, inside Paladins.

Blocks
Place and break blocks from the Shooting Range. A Minecraft server decides what happens, and Paladins draws the result.

Install in three steps

  1. Download the installer

    Download RealmSuite-version-Setup.exe.

  2. Run it

    It installs for your Windows user, without asking for admin rights, into AppData\Local\Programs\RealmSuite, and adds RealmSuite to the Start menu. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the program is not code-signed: choose More info, then Run anyway. To remove it later, use Installed apps in Windows' Settings; it asks whether to keep your settings, skin profiles and sound packs.

  3. Open RealmSuite and press Play

    The installer opens it at the end, and the Start menu has it after that. The Play page shows the game it found and says Ready to play; if it found none, point it at Paladins.exe in Settings. Press Play: Paladins starts and RealmSuite loads into it. Paladins signs in as usual unless you choose otherwise under Settings, Sign in. In the game, F1 opens the menu, and you can change that key in the launcher's Settings.

Prefer no installer? Download the zip instead, extract it to a folder you will keep (not Program Files), and run RealmSuite.exe in it.

Updates

Checking
The launcher checks for a new release when it starts and every few hours while it is open, and downloads only the files that changed. It verifies the release's signature and each file's hash before it installs anything.
Installing
An update installs only while Paladins is closed. When one is ready, the Play page offers Restart to Update: close the game, then press it. An update that is ready while you play installs the next time the launcher starts.
Uninstall
Installed apps in Windows' Settings. With the zip: close Paladins and the launcher, then delete the RealmSuite folder.

Questions

Is it safe?

RealmSuite works by loading code into the running Paladins process; that is how it can add a scoreboard, change skins and draw its own menus. It is an independent project, not made or endorsed by Hi-Rez Studios, and no one can promise how the game's rules or its anti-cheat will treat any modification. Use it at your own risk.

What we can say:

  • Every release is signed. The launcher checks the signature and each file's hash before it installs an update, and installs only while Paladins is closed.
  • You choose what runs. Every mod, and every feature of a mod, has its own switch, and deleting the folder removes everything.
  • RealmAudio writes nothing to the game's folder, and RealmForge's skins exist only on your screen: that mod sends nothing to the server.
How do I sign in to Paladins?

Play starts Paladins and signs in the way you chose under Settings, Sign in. Steam account: Paladins signs in through Steam. A saved account: one you added, with its password encrypted on this PC; Paladins writes its sign-in into its own log files. In the game: Paladins' own sign-in, or the one it remembers. Out of the box it is In the game.

Can I start Paladins from Steam or Epic myself?

Play always adds RealmSuite. A game you start from Steam, Epic or Paladins.exe gets RealmSuite only if Join games started elsewhere is switched on under Settings, Launching; otherwise it runs without it.

Which Paladins installs work?

Steam, Epic and the Hi-Rez standalone install on Windows. The launcher finds them from the stores' records; if yours is somewhere else, choose its Paladins.exe in the launcher's Settings.

How do I uninstall it?

Use Installed apps in Windows' Settings; it asks whether to keep your settings, skin profiles and sound packs. With the zip, close Paladins and the launcher and delete the RealmSuite folder: your settings, logs and saved skin profiles are inside it, so they go with it.

Will other players see my skins?

No. Custom skins, colors and effects exist only on your screen. The mod sends nothing to the server, so everyone else sees your normal skins, and you see theirs as they are.

A mod stopped working. What now?

A feature that fails stops on its own and the menu says why. Switch the mod or the feature off and on again, and if it stops again, tell us (see below) with the log: Open Logs in the launcher's Settings shows the folder, and Logs/host.log is the one to send.

The launcher says an update is unfinished. What now?

Close Paladins and press Try Again. If that does not help, run the latest release's RealmSuite-version-Setup.exe again (or, for a zip install, extract its zip over your RealmSuite folder); this repairs the program and keeps your settings, logs and saved skin profiles.

Where do I ask a question or report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub and say which mod and what you did, with Logs/host.log if the game or a mod failed. You can also ask in the Discord.
